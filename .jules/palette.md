## 2025-05-14 - Visual Feedback for Clipboard Actions
**Learning:** Users lack confidence in silent background actions. Providing immediate visual feedback (e.g., changing button text from "Copiar" to "Copiado" with a checkmark) significantly improves the perceived reliability of the "Copy to Clipboard" feature.
**Action:** Always implement a brief state change or notification for clipboard-related interactions.
