## 2025-05-23 - Improve refinement workflow and accessibility
**Learning:** Selecting a GEM in the dashboard should automatically focus the refinement textarea to facilitate immediate interaction. Providing keyboard shortcuts like `Ctrl+Enter` significantly improves the efficiency of power users. Decorative emojis should be hidden from screen readers to reduce noise.
**Action:** Implement auto-focus on GEM selection, add `Ctrl+Enter` support for submission, and use `aria-hidden` on decorative elements.
