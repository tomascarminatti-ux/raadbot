## 2025-05-15 - [Collapsible Glassmorphism Drawers]
**Learning:** For background data like 'Live Logs', a collapsible glassmorphism drawer at the bottom of the screen preserves screen real estate while maintaining visual consistency with the dark theme. Using `translate-y` with Tailwind transitions provides a smooth, "industrial" feel suitable for Raadbot.
**Action:** Use the `translate-y-[calc(height-header)]` pattern for future footer-docked utility drawers to ensure they are accessible but non-intrusive.
