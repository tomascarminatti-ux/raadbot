## 2025-05-15 - Improving Power User Efficiency and Accessibility
**Learning:** Adding keyboard shortcuts (like Ctrl+Enter) and quick-action buttons (like Copy to Clipboard) significantly improves the experience for power users without cluttering the UI. Explicit focus-visible styles are crucial for keyboard-only users in industrial-style dashboards.
**Action:** Always include keyboard shortcut hints in placeholders and ensure every interactive element has a visible focus state.
