## 2026-07-07 - Accessibility and Copy Feedback
**Learning:** Adding keyboard accessibility (tabindex="0" and focus rings) to scrollable containers like <pre> blocks is crucial for keyboard-only users who otherwise cannot scroll long content. Visual feedback for clipboard actions (like changing an icon to a checkmark) significantly improves the perceived responsiveness of the UI.
**Action:** Always ensure scrollable areas have a focus state and provide immediate, temporary visual confirmation for copy-to-clipboard actions.
