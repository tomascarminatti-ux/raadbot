## 2025-05-15 - [Copy to Clipboard with Feedback and Accessibility]
**Learning:** Providing immediate visual and ARIA feedback (temporary checkmark icon/text and updating `aria-label`) for clipboard operations significantly improves perceived responsiveness and accessibility. Hiding decorative elements from screen readers reduces noise in complex industrial dashboards.
**Action:** Always include success states for copy actions and ensure decorative icons are `aria-hidden="true"`.
